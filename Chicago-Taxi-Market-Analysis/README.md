# Chicago Taxi Market Analysis

## Business Problem

Zuber, a new ride-sharing company preparing to enter the Chicago market, needed to understand the competitive landscape, geographic demand patterns, and the impact of weather conditions on taxi trip duration.

The objective was to analyze competitor activity and customer demand to identify market opportunities and develop data-driven recommendations for the company's market entry strategy.

## Tools & Technologies

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SciPy, SQL, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Data Visualization, Statistical Analysis, and Hypothesis Testing.

## Project Highlights

- Retrieved and analyzed taxi trip data using SQL queries.
- Examined the distribution of trips across taxi companies to assess market concentration and competitive positioning.
- Identified the most popular drop-off locations and geographic demand patterns.
- Investigated the relationship between rainy weather and taxi trip duration.
- Conducted statistical hypothesis testing to evaluate differences in trip duration under different weather conditions.
- Translated analytical findings into strategic recommendations for market entry, pricing, and fleet management.

## Key Findings

### Competitive Market Analysis

- Eleven companies, representing approximately 17% of the market, accounted for 75.95% of total trips.
- Flash Cab was the market leader with 19,558 trips, exceeding the second-largest competitor by 8,136 trips.
- Twenty-nine smaller companies, each with fewer than 100 trips, collectively represented only 0.41% of the market.
- These findings indicate a highly concentrated market with established competitors and significant entry challenges.

### Geographic Demand Patterns

- The Loop was the leading destination, with an average of 10,727 trips.
- Demand was concentrated around the financial district and central commercial areas.
- The analysis identified opportunities to differentiate through coverage in less-served neighborhoods.

### Weather Impact on Trip Duration

- Trips on rainy days were approximately 377 seconds (6.3 minutes) longer than trips on non-rainy days, according to the analysis.
- This finding highlights the importance of weather conditions in operational planning and service efficiency.

## Strategic Recommendations for Zuber

### Market Entry Strategy

- Prioritize the Loop and surrounding neighborhoods during the initial launch.
- Begin with intensive coverage in three to five key neighborhoods before expanding.
- Identify underserved routes and time periods to find opportunities beyond established competitors.

### Competitive Differentiation

- Develop specialized services that address customer needs not fully covered by larger competitors.
- Invest in an intuitive application and shorter response times.
- Use personalized customer service as a competitive advantage.

### Weather-Based Pricing and Operations

- Consider dynamic pricing adjustments during rainy conditions to account for longer trip durations.
- Communicate pricing changes transparently to customers.
- Explore driver incentives during adverse weather conditions.
- Improve fleet availability and vehicle positioning based on weather forecasts and demand patterns.
