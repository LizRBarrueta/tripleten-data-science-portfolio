# Megaline — Telecom Plan Analysis and Recommendation

## Project Overview

Megaline, a telecommunications provider, offers two prepaid plans: Surf and Ultimate.

This project consists of two complementary analyses. The first investigates customer behavior and revenue patterns to determine which plan generates higher revenue. The second develops a machine learning model to recommend the most suitable plan for customers based on their usage patterns.

## Part 1 — Exploratory Data Analysis and Statistical Analysis

### Objectives

- Analyze data from 500 Megaline customers during 2018.
- Compare revenue generated by the Surf and Ultimate plans.
- Investigate differences in customer revenue between the NY-NJ region and other regions.
- Perform statistical hypothesis testing to validate the findings.
- Provide business recommendations based on the results.

### Key Findings

**Plan Comparison: Surf vs. Ultimate**

- Ultimate generated approximately $11.61 more per user per month than Surf.
- The difference was statistically significant (p-value = 0.0000).
- Ultimate generated approximately 21% more revenue per user than Surf.

**Regional Analysis: NY-NJ vs. Other Regions**

- Customers in other regions generated approximately $5.30 more per user per month than customers in NY-NJ.
- The difference was statistically significant (p-value = 0.035).

### Business Recommendations

- Prioritize advertising investments in promoting the Ultimate plan.
- Develop region-specific strategies to address the revenue gap in NY-NJ.
- Consider encouraging Surf customers to upgrade to Ultimate.
- Focus additional marketing efforts on regions generating higher revenue.

## Part 2 — Machine Learning: Plan Recommendation

### Objectives

- Develop a binary classification model to recommend either the Surf or Ultimate plan.
- Analyze customer usage patterns and identify differences between plan users.
- Train and optimize classification models.
- Achieve an accuracy of at least 75% on the test dataset.

### Methodology

- Prepared customer usage data for classification.
- Compared customer behavior across usage variables.
- Trained and evaluated multiple classification algorithms.
- Optimized model hyperparameters to improve predictive performance.
- Compared the selected model against a simple baseline strategy.

### Results

<badge color="success">Target achieved</badge>

**Selected Model: Random Forest Classifier**

- **Accuracy:** 80.25%
- **Required threshold:** 75%
- **Improvement over baseline:** 11.82 percentage points
- **Configuration:** `n_estimators=50`

The Random Forest model achieved the required accuracy threshold, correctly classifying approximately 8 out of 10 customers.

### Business Recommendations

- Implement the Random Forest model in the customer recommendation system.
- Monitor model performance using new customer data.
- Retrain the model periodically to maintain predictive accuracy.
