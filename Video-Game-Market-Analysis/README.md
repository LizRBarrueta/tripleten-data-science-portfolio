# Video Game Market Analysis

## Business Problem

An online video game retailer wanted to identify patterns that determine the success of video games across different platforms, genres, and geographic markets. The objective was to analyze historical sales data, user and critic reviews, and regional preferences to support strategic planning for 2017.

## Tools & Technologies

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SciPy, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Data Visualization, Statistical Analysis, and Hypothesis Testing.

## Project Highlights

- Analyzed historical video game sales data to identify market trends and changes over time.
- Examined platform popularity and lifecycle patterns, including the emergence and decline of major gaming platforms.
- Compared genre performance and identified differences in regional preferences.
- Developed regional customer profiles for North America, Europe, and Japan based on platform, genre, rating, and sales patterns.
- Investigated the relationship between critic and user scores and total game sales.
- Conducted statistical hypothesis tests to compare user ratings across platforms and genres.

## Key Findings

### Market Trends and Platform Lifecycle

- Historical sales peaked in 2008, reaching 651.50 million units.
- The 2006–2010 period represented the market's strongest sales cycle.
- A second sales peak occurred in 2013, coinciding with the launch of a new console generation.
- By 2016, major platforms showed an overall decline in sales.
- Platform lifecycles generally lasted approximately 8–10 years, while PC remained active throughout the period analyzed.

### Genre and Regional Preferences

- Action was the leading genre historically and in 2016.
- Shooters became the second-largest genre by sales during 2014–2016, surpassing Sports.
- Role-Playing games had a particularly strong presence in Japan.
- North American and European markets showed stronger preferences for Action and Shooter games.
- Japan showed a stronger preference for Nintendo platforms and handheld gaming.

### Reviews and Sales

- Critic scores showed a moderate positive correlation with sales (0.335).
- User scores showed almost no linear correlation with sales (0.010).
- Games receiving both critic and user reviews generated higher aggregate sales than games without reviews, according to the project's analysis.

## Statistical Hypothesis Testing

Two statistical comparisons were conducted:

- **PC vs. Xbox One user ratings:** PC games received ratings 0.541 points higher on average than Xbox One games.
- **Action vs. Sports user ratings:** The analysis examined whether users rated these two genres differently.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the historical analysis, the project proposed the following strategic considerations for 2017:

- Focus on platform transitions and emerging opportunities as established platforms approach the end of their lifecycle.
- Consider regional differences when planning game releases and marketing strategies.
- Prioritize quality and development opportunities in the Shooter genre, which showed strong sales performance.
- Consider critic reviews as a relevant factor when evaluating potential game success.
- Adapt product and marketing strategies to the distinct preferences of North American, European, and Japanese audiences.
